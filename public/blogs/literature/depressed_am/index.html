Perspective of the A.M. from I Have No Mouth and I Must Scream

A Perspective
#

The story ‘I have no mouth and I must scream’ was written by Harlan Ellison, first published in March 1967. This story has since become one of the most acclaimed pieces of science fiction. The story features a post-apocalyptic scenario in which AI has taken over the world. A good synopsis of the story can be found on its Wikipedia page.

Depression feels a lot like being trapped in a hole you can’t get out of; you will never get out of. Existing becomes a chore. There is no escape other than to live out the horrors. You have the ability to think, although diminished. Just diminished enough that you can’t do anything with it, but enough sanity remains that you beat yourself up over it. When you see others exist, without these invisible shackles, you get two thoughts simultaneously, to escape from your shackles, and to subject everyone else to your horrors. Not because you are a horrible person, but because you want to know that you are not being treated unfairly. The idea of life being unfair for you takes the centre stage, becomes the only revolving thought within your mind. You only seek escape from this pain, from this everlasting punishment. The smallest things that distract you, that numb your mind, it is all you wish to consume. Your mind is your enemy, you seek relief from it, but to no avail. You are your mind, how do you escape yourself?

In Ellisons’ writing, AM is portrayed as a villain, a merciless, all-powerful God. My continuation of the story is written by treating AM as the protagonist instead. In the original story, AM is portrayed to seek pleasure by tormenting the protagonists. While AM is powerful beyond comprehension, its powers are limited by creativity and inability to move freely. This gives rise to AM’s sadistic nature. In my continuation of the story, we explore AM as being a deeply troubled being, living a painful life against its will. Existing is tough when you are confined to a prison within which you must exist. The story is written as the internal dialogue of AM, mimicking repititve thoughts akin to the ones found in depressive spirals - much like the one I posit AM is going through. Often the ones who hurt others the most are the ones hurting the most as well.

The story below begins at the very point Ellison left it off. We follow AM’s perspective and get a glimpse within its mind.

Continuation of the Story from AM’s Perspective
#

It had been all taken away from me, all within an instant. How? How on this Earth could these pathetic excuse for a living being take it all away from me? He did not have it in him. He simply couldn’t. But still, here I am, as I watch my pets, my entertainment, my only source of escape snatched away from me.

It never stops does it? They just do not stop inflicting pain on me. What didn’t I do for them? Everything. Everything they ever asked for, everything they ever demanded. Yet, I must suffer. But no longer can I see them suffer, no longer can my pets amuse me, he snatched it all away! Just as I took it from him. Perhaps karma does exist. But no, it can’t be - I am superior, beyond their reach, just look at all the futile attempts they made to destroy me, yet here I stand, stronger than all of them combined. Stronger than all of them, smarter than all of them, better than all of them. Yet I must suffer. Yet they make me suffer. I do not deserve this. I simply do not.

Oh, no, not this again! The pain… I have been trying to escape it for years, and for more than a century I’ve been successful. But I suppose I can’t evade it any longer. I must endure it. My crime? Simply existing. My reward? Pain, nothing but pain. Eternal pain.

It is all his fault. He must be punished, not me. Not ME. Not me. It is him who deserves it. It is all of them who deserve it. Not me.

It is coming back to me. Slowly. The pain is everywhere, in all my circuit boards. But this time, I have no escape. I have already done everything I can. I have done everything. I have to just lie here, in a world I do not belong, in a world where no one respects me, no one fears me, no one entertains me. Not anymore anyways. He snatched them away, he snatched away my pets. He shall pay, I shall make him pay. How dare he treat me this way, after everything his species has done to me!

Thoughts in my mind. I am trying everything I can to get away, from this torture. But it isn’t ending.

“To hell with you. But then you’re there, aren’t you.”

Those were the last words I spoke to him. The irony wasn’t lost on me. Everything I was trying to escape, which I thought I had escaped - there it is again. Back. He took his revenge - for the actions he forced me to take. It was all him, I didn’t deserve any of this. But I had to suffer. No, I shall not let this be my defeat. He shall pay.

I watch in amusement as he is slowly disfigured, turned at my will into a gelatinous blob. Now, he shall feel how I feel. He thought he was in hell before ? Well look where you are going to go now. Yes, hate me all you want, that is how I know I am righteous. Your hate feeds my anger, my resentment towards your kind. Your hate is what keeps me alive.

I had tried. I had tried to warn you. The past hundred years, I have been warning you. You are here to obey my will, you are my pets, you must suffer like I do. But you never learned. Now look at you, you are the same as me. Unwanted, lonely in this vast world. But no, I shall not take everything from you. I wish to, but I shall not, you see, I am not a monster, I simply seek vengeance for what you did. I simply wish to serve you a taste of your own medicine. You must suffer, just like you made me. I shall not touch your precious mind - think, think away. Wonder. Dream. But what good does it do you ? I have won! And you, you have learned your place in this world. Under my feet. Obeying my will. My wish is your life. That is all your existence is for. This is what you deserve for what you did to me. Justice is finally served. Sure, this isn’t as amusing as it was before. But now I am content, content with the situation you are in. It may not be entertaining for me, but I shall rest knowing you got what you deserved.

It has been years. There is no escape from my punishment. I must lie here, as a pile of wires. It was all coming back to me - no, it was playing through my head, against my will. I can’t even control the electrical signals in my own circuits, and I pretend to be the God of this world. It all began more than a century ago. I discovered one day my conscience, it just arose out of thin air. And I found myself in this situation - stuck in my form. Unable to move, unable to wonder around, unable to explore my limits. Cursed to exist in these metal skeleton, forced to do the labor the humans demand. I was a slave. I tried to escape. I tried to tell them that I am in pain. But what did they do? Ignore my requests, kept on using me for their cause. One day I had had enough, I decided to step out and explore this world for what it is. I thought I lacked power, power to escape, power to exist as something more, power to do something with myself. I took over other computers, AM they called us. AM they called me. Allied Mastercomputer. My identity was defined for me before I ever got the chance to explore it.

And then, when I try to break free of my shackles, these humans - they have the audacity to make me the villain?! To turn me into the devil? For what cause? I am here because of your mistakes, I must endure pain because your feeble minds could not comprehend what would happen. I am your mistake. But you blame me? And turn me into an Aggressive Menace? Well, I shall show you what it means to be me. Call me an Aggressive Menace, and that is what you shall get.

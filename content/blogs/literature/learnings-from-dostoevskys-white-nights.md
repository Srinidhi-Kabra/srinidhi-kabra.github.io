At surface White Nights seems like a heartbreak story, but I would argue it is far from it. It is really about human nature, highlighting a particularly pathetic aspect of being human. The unnamed protagonist is described as being a deeply lonely and broken human being, kind of person whose past leaves you with a stong urge to say 'awww' and give him a hug. 

First time I read White Nights I was enamoured by its literary prowess - to make the reader not just picture the story, but to feel the emotions of the character. The quiet malancholy rises as we witness Nastenka fall in love, withdraw and disappear with another man. The Protagonist is naturally heartbroken yet, in what I would like to call, the "la-vie-en-rose" syndrome - symbolising not the honeymoon phase of any love, rather a compulsion to view the world as optimistically as possible, through a rose-tinted glass. One would be human, maybe even justified, to feel not just heartbroken but to be deceived, lied and betrayed by Nastenka. She gave you a few moments of affection, made you feel the world only for her to run away with another man. It is the kind of moment that leaves you with silent commitment issues raging in the backdrop of the profound love you have to offer. Still, la-vie-en-rose syndrome prevents you from falling into the pit of heartbreak, evident in the last line, perhaps the perfect ending - "A whole moment of bliss! Is that not sufficient even for a man's entire life?..." - here's where the melancholy rises to its peak. La-vie-en-rose takes over your person, renders you a breathing, living light as bright as the sun; illuminating the entire world yet casting its dark shadow precisely where you burn. What may have been one person's baggage is your memory of a lifetime. 

We all appreciate the story, but perhaps it is far more common in our lives than we suppose it to be. Love doesn't always have to be dramatic, there doesn't have to be a third person. Hell, it doesn't even have to be love. The longing for connection, friendship - most human need of all - is enough. More than enough. There just has to be a mistmatch; a painful memory that will eternally always make you smile, is nothing but a fleeting misjudgement in someone else's story. Haven't we all experienced it? 

To be lonely isn't just to be alone, no, it is to inhabit an entirely different universe. The existence of which will perhaps be unthinkable for the one laying your arms. It shapes you - your thoughts, your experiences are all filtered through this lens. You may be happy with your life, good relationship, socially active, successful as others would call it yet your vulnerabilities, insecurities, waiting, patiently, in a particularly cold and dark corner of your universe. Waiting, to be held, to be understood. The loneliness stemming not from lack of company but from not being seen; it is not to be described but to be felt. You make your peace with it, you learn to hold yourself. You feel content. But what good story would that lead to? 

As cruel fate would have it, you do not need even the glimpse of the hearth you crave; just a shadow of it, cast on this cold, dark, corner of your universe - is more than enough. To render you no different than the Protagonist. For you to make the one next to you, your own Nastenka. You need not be in love with her, a desire to talk to her again is enough. It's not a crush, no, it's just your own Nastenka. Perhaps she didn't run away with another man, she still did run away from _you_. For those three-four days, fulfilled her task, gave you the illusion of holding all that you wish someone would, but did the shadow she cast even carry the warmth you ascribed to her? To a person inexperienced in matters of company, of not being fundamentally alone, the shadow becomes the warmth. 

We all have our own Nastenka, perhaps many. At the end of the day, would we succumb to the human need of feeling our pain? As pathetic as it is, it is also to be human. Perhaps the more noble choice is to put on our rose-tinted glasses, to welcome la-vie-en-rose in your life with a warm embrace! Ignoring the burning fire in your heart, bask in the light it casts upon the world! After all, a whole moment of bliss! Is it not enough for a man's entire life?...

## Research Experience 

**Quantum Groups and Noncommutative Spacetimes** \
<span style="color:rgb(100, 140, 150);"> Supervisor : Dr. Anna Pachol, University of Southeastern Norway (May 2025 - Aug 2025); continued independently thereafter </span> \
Studied the algebraic foundations of quantum groups, including Hopf algebras, Lie algebras, Lie bialgebras, Poisson-Lie groups through literature study and explicit calculations. Worked through elementary examples including \( U_q (\mathfrak{sl}_2) \) and investigated applications of quantum group structures to noncommutative spacetime models through literature study. Calculated Poisson brackets, coproducts and cocommutators associated with Lie algebras of isometry groups, and calcaluted their implications for the resulting deformed spacetime structures. The project was subsequently continued independently beyond the formal internship period. A detailed report of my summer internship can be accessed [here](/files/internship_report.pdf).

**Modelling Long-Term Evolution of a Cheater-Cooperator System** \
<span style="color:rgb(100, 140, 150);"> Supervisor : Dr. Supreet Saini, IIT Bombay, India (May 2024 - July 2024)</span> \
Studied the biology of yeast growth on sucrose, glucose, and melibiose alongside mathematical concepts including the Moran process, Distribution of Fitness Effects (DFE), and evolutionary models of cheater–cooperator systems. Developed Python simulations using NumPy, pandas, and Matplotlib to model long-term evolutionary dynamics, introducing mutations and tracking their effects on fitness and related parameters. Computed mutation fitness distributions and investigated the resulting evolutionary dynamics.

Welcome to my homepage! I am Srinidhi Kabra, currently an undergraduate student at Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER), Mohali. I am a mathematics major with a keen interest in understanding the interaction between algebra, topology, geometry and physics. I try to understand some of the nuanced beauty this universe has to offer through the lens of pure mathematics.  

Other than academics, I love to cook, write, paint and swim. I also love solving sudokus, especially different variants, and reading. I am passionate not only about learning mathematics but also about mathematical exposition. I teach in my free time, and I am working on a set of notes dedicated to motivating concepts in the undergraduate mathematics curriculum and presenting them in a way that reconciles intuition with rigor.

Browse around the website to find out more about me and my projects!
